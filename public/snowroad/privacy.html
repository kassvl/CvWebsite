SnowRoad Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

SnowRoad is made by Kadirhan Emre Memis, the controller of your data. Contact: kadirhanemrememis@gmail.com.

You use SnowRoad without an account. Your rides, routes and saved resorts stay on your phone. Something reaches our server in only two cases: what a team needs to work, once you create or join one; and your answer, if you answer the question on the Season Pass card.

On your phone

  • Ride recordings: time, distance, speed and the GPS route. We use your location in the background so a ride keeps recording with the screen locked; the route is still stored only on your phone.
  • Check-ins, badges, favourite resorts, your language and region.
  • Your team name: also saved on the phone so you don't have to type it again. While you're in a team it is on our server too (see below).
  • Apple Watch: the watch app shows your team (names, colours, positions, height and speed). It gets this directly from your phone, not from our server. While the find screen is open, the watch uses its own compass and GPS to point you to your teammate; the watch's position stays on the watch and is not sent to the phone or the team.
  • Lock screen (iPhone): while you ride in a team, the lock screen and the Dynamic Island show a small radar of your teammates (names, colours, distance, direction and height). Your phone draws it from the team data it already has; nothing extra goes to our server or to Apple. Anyone who can see your lock screen sees it too. Swipe it away for that ride, or turn it off in Settings > SnowRoad > Live Activities.

Apart from your team name while you're in a team, we can't see any of this. Deleting the app removes it from the phone. If your phone makes backups (Google or iCloud), a copy can stay in your backup and come back when you reinstall.

On our server, in team mode

When you create or join a team (on the ride tab), your phone creates an anonymous user on our server in Frankfurt (Supabase). We never ask for your e-mail, phone number or real name.

  • Team code and the name you type: kept so your teammates see you in the list. When you leave, your name is removed from the list at once. A team stops accepting new members after 48 hours and is deleted entirely after 7 days without use.
  • Meeting point: if someone in the team sets one, it lasts as long as the team.
  • Your live position, height and speed: sent only while you ride, and only to your teammates. They are not stored on the server; they pass through live and are gone.
  • Wrong code attempts: logged with a time, to stop people guessing team codes.

The Season Pass question

If you pick a price on the Season Pass card and send it, your phone creates an anonymous user even if you're not in a team. We keep the price range you picked, the currency and the app language with that anonymous id. Answering is optional.

How long we keep it

  • Team, names in it and the meeting point: deleted after 7 days in which nobody joins, leaves, sets a meeting point or reopens the team. A team stops accepting new members 48 hours after it is created.
  • Wrong code attempts: deleted after 24 hours.
  • Anonymous id and your Season Pass answer: deleted after 400 days in which you did not open the app with a connection or send a new answer (so you keep them if you come back next season).
  • Whenever you want: in the app, open the language button on the Explore tab and tap "Delete my data". That removes your team membership, your Season Pass answer and your anonymous id from our server at once, and everything SnowRoad keeps on your phone. It needs an internet connection and can't run while a ride is being recorded; in either case nothing is deleted and the app says so.
  • Server logs: Supabase keeps technical logs (including IP addresses) under its own retention rules, which we don't control.

Legal basis

  • Team mode: providing the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Wrong-code log: keeping the service secure, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Season Pass answer: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Other services

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): receives the resort's coordinates, never yours.
  • Exchange rates (Frankfurter): receives no personal data.
  • Resort photos (Wikimedia): photos load from Wikimedia's servers.
  • Maps: Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android. When the map shows your own position, Apple's and Google's terms apply.

As with any internet request, the service you reach sees your IP address and may keep it briefly in its logs.

What we don't use

No ads, no advertising id, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Leaving a team removes your name from its list straight away, and "Delete my data" in the app erases everything we hold about you (see above). For anything else, write to kadirhanemrememis@gmail.com. Your data is tied to an anonymous id, not your name, so to find it we may ask for your team code and the name you used. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be on this page and in the app.
