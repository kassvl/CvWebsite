SnowRoad Help

SnowRoad helps you choose a ski resort, record your riding and find your friends on the slopes. It needs no account and works in English, Turkish and Polish.

Contact

Write to kadirhanemrememis@gmail.com. If something went wrong, tell us your phone model, your iOS version and what you were doing; a screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

  • The ride stops recording when I lock the phone. Allow location access ("While Using the App" is enough) and keep Precise Location on in Settings > SnowRoad > Location. Start the ride on the Ride tab before you lock the phone. Low Power Mode can make GPS less frequent.
  • How does team mode work? On the Ride tab, one of you taps "Start a team" and shares the code; the others tap "Join a team" and enter it. While you ride, everyone in the team sees each other on the map. Team mode needs a mobile data connection.
  • The radar doesn't appear on my lock screen. It shows while you record a ride in a team, on iOS 16.2 or later. Check that Live Activities are on in Settings > SnowRoad.
  • Apple Watch. The watch app shows your team while your iPhone is in one, and points you to a teammate on the find screen.
  • Ticket prices. Each price shows its source and date. Resorts change prices during the season, so check the resort's own site before you buy.
  • Deleting your data. On the Explore tab, tap the language button at the top (it shows EN, TR or PL), then "Delete my data". This removes everything on the phone and what we keep on the server. You can also ask us by e-mail.

Privacy

Your rides and routes stay on your phone. The privacy policy explains what goes to our server in team mode and for how long.
